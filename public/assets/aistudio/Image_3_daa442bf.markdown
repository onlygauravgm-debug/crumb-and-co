## Brand & Style

The design system establishes a warm, sensory, and tactile digital presence for an artisanal contemporary bakery based in India. It balances European viennoiserie heritage with the vibrant, comforting hospitality of modern urban Indian cafe culture. The interface evokes the comfort of oven-fresh sourdough, roasted coffee beans, and warm morning sun cast across limestone countertops.

The design movement mixes **Warm Organic Minimalism** with **Tactile / Soft Neomorphic Nuances**. Visual surfaces avoid stark synthetic whites and cold dark greys; instead, they rely on layers of clotted cream, unbleached flour, whipped butter, and deep roasted cocoa. The emotional goal is immediate salivation, intuitive ease, and cozy approachability while retaining high culinary craft standards.

## Colors

The palette is rooted in the baking process: flour, caramelized crusts, golden butter, and deep chocolate.

- **Canvas & Surface Tier:**
  - Base Background Canvas: `#FDFBF7` (Unbleached warm ivory)
  - Card & Container Surface: `#FFFFFF` (Pure warm white for crisp containment)
  - Muted Container Surface: `#F7F3EB` (Soft stone cream for nested elements, secondary panels)
- **Content & Text:**
  - Primary Typography: `#2C1810` (Espresso roasted bean — high contrast, ultra-rich)
  - Secondary Typography & Subtle Labels: `#5D4037` (Muted dark walnut)
  - Decorative Borders & Dividers: `#EADBCE` (Warm biscuit border)
- **Interactive Accents:**
  - Primary CTA / Accent: `#D96B43` (Rich terracotta / hearth crust)
  - Primary Hover / Pressed: `#C85A32` (Caramelized terracotta)
  - Secondary Accent / Highlights: `#E9C46A` (Warm churned butter)
  - Tertiary Accent / Warm Badges: `#F4A261` (Honeyed apricot)
- **Status & Feedback:**
  - Success: `#588157` (Fresh sage herb)
  - Warning: `#E09F3E` (Mustard seed)
  - Error: `#BC4749` (Spiced red cherry)

## Typography

The type system blends the literary grace of **Newsreader** for headings with the warm, open geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for interfaces and body copy.

- **Editorial Headlines (Newsreader):** Brings warmth, authenticity, and European bistro heritage without feeling aloof or dated. Used for hero headlines, product titles, category headers, and pull quotes.
- **Functional Body & Microcopy (Plus Jakarta Sans):** Offers balanced proportions, slightly rounded terminals, and clear numerals (ideal for pricing in ₹ INR and nutritional specs).
- **Styling Rules:** Editorial headings can leverage italic styles sparingly for highlighted culinary terminology (e.g., *fermentation*, *laminated*). Never set serif headings in all-caps.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy follows a **fluid-grid rhythm with generous culinary whitespace**, allowing photography of golden pastries and rustic breads to breathe.

- **Grid Architecture:**
  - **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column layout, max-width `1280px`, `4rem` outer margin, `2rem` gutters.
  - **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column layout, `2rem` outer margin, `1.5rem` gutters.
  - **Mobile (320px - 767px):** 4-column layout, `1rem` outer margin, `1rem` gutters.
- **Spatial Rhythm:** Elements follow an 8pt baseline scale. Card interiors prioritize top-and-bottom breathing room (`1.5rem` to `2rem`) to emulate printed cafe menus. Negative space around high-value items conveys artisanal restraint rather than fast-casual density.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth is achieved through **ambient warm shadows** and **tactile tone-on-tone layering**, completely avoiding stark synthetic black dropshadows.

- **Surface Tonal Stacking:**
  - Background base canvas sits at `#FDFBF7`.
  - Floating items (e.g., cards, elevated selectors) sit on `#FFFFFF` with warm undertone borders (`1px solid #EADBCE`).
  - Active drawers and bottom navigation sheets use `#F7F3EB` to ground user focus.
- **Ambient Shadow System (Umber & Cinnamon tinted):**
  - **Resting Elevation (Cards, Badges):** `0px 4px 20px -2px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.05), 0px 2px 6px -1px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.03)`
  - **Lifted Elevation (Hovered Cards, Dropdowns):** `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.08), 0px 4px 12px -2px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.04)`
  - **Overlay Elevation (Modals, Sticky Cart Tray):** `0px 24px 48px -8px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.12), 0px 8px 16px -4px rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.06)`
- **Atmospheric Backdrops:** Mobile bottom sheets and dialog overlays use `rgba(44, 24, 16, 0.4)` paired with an `8px` gaussian blur.

## Shapes

The shape system adopts a pill-infused, soft-sculpted geometry reflecting the puffy contour of leavened dough and smooth ceramic kitchenware.

- **Standard Base Shapes (`roundedness: 2`):** Primary radius is `0.5rem` (8px).
- **Macro Components:**
  - Product Cards, Modals, Promotional Banners: Utilize `rounded-2xl` (`1.25rem` / 20px) to `rounded-3xl` (`1.75rem` / 28px) for an organic, artisanal feel.
  - Interactive Action Items (Buttons, Chips, Quantity Adjusters): Utilize full pill curvature (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for effortless touch interactions on mobile devices.
- **Image Masking:** Product thumbnails should always maintain smooth squircle or pill-rounded corners to complement pastry contours.

## Components

- **Buttons:**
  - *Primary CTA:* Solid `#D96B43` background, `#FFFFFF` text, `rounded-full`, vertical padding `0.875rem`, horizontal `1.75rem`. Hover state warms to `#C85A32` with an ambient warm shadow.
  - *Secondary Button:* `#F7F3EB` background with `#2C1810` text, subtle `1px solid #EADBCE` stroke. Hover shifts to `#EADBCE`.
  - *Tertiary / Text Link:* Newsreader italic or Plus Jakarta Sans bold with an animated warm apricot (`#F4A261`) underline.
- **Chips & Badges:**
  - Dietary badges (Eggless, Vegan, Sourdough, Gluten-Friendly): Pill-shaped (`rounded-full`), padding `0.25rem 0.75rem`.
  - Bestseller / Seasonal Tags: Tinted `#F4A261` with `#2C1810` high-contrast typography.
- **Cards (Pastry & Bread Items):**
  - Crisp warm white background (`#FFFFFF`) framed by `1px solid #EADBCE`.
  - Border radius `rounded-2xl`.
  - High-aspect photography top section, generous padding (`1.25rem`) for title, short origin note, price in bold Indian Rupee format (`₹`), and an integrated pill-shaped "Add +" trigger.
- **Input Fields & Form Controls:**
  - Text Inputs: Background `#FDFBF7`, border `1.5px solid #EADBCE`, border radius `1rem`. Active focus shifts border to `#D96B43` with an outer ring of `rgba(217, 107, 67, 0.2)`. Placeholder text in `#8D7A73`.
  - Checkboxes & Radios: Generously proportioned (`20px`), circular for radios, smoothed rounded-rectangles for checkboxes. Checked state fills `#D96B43` with `#FFFFFF` checkmark.
- **Lists & Order Trays:**
  - Cart item lists feature divided items separated by soft dotted or `#EADBCE` hairline dividers. Quantity steppers (`-` / `+`) are encapsulated pill units in `#F7F3EB`.
- **Specialty Bakery Components:**
  - *Oven Timer / Freshness Indicator:* Pill badge featuring a gentle pulsing terracotta dot and baking timestamp (e.g., "Freshly baked at 8:30 AM").
  - *Pickup / Delivery Switcher:* Floating pill segment control with smooth sliding `#FFFFFF` indicator against a `#F7F3EB` background.
